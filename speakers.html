Speakers

Distinct voices. New perspectives.

MARKA London brings together influential thinkers, creators and leaders shaping culture, design, luxury and the way we live today. Expect unexpected encounters, fresh perspectives and conversations that stay with you long after the day ends.

Alfhild Külper

Textile Artist & Designer

For Alfhild Külper, softness is more than a material quality — it is a way of seeing the world. The Swedish-born, Amsterdam-based artist transforms wool, memory and emotion into tactile landscapes that blur the boundaries between art and design. Working with leftover yarns and surplus wool, she gives forgotten materials new life, making sustainability part of her creative language. A former Head of Design at Viktor & Rolf, her work explores craft, human touch, comfort and connection.

Dylan Jones

Editor · Author · Cultural Curator

For decades, Dylan Jones has had a front-row seat to British culture and helped shape the conversation around it. From fashion and music to art, lifestyle, politics and society, his world has always been about the people, ideas and encounters that define an era. An editor, author and cultural connector with an instinct for what and who matters next.

Luca Molinari

Architect, Critic & Curator

For Luca Molinari, architecture is never simply about buildings — it is about people, culture and the way we imagine our future. An architect, critic and curator, he has spent his career exploring how cities, spaces and institutions can carry memory while responding to a changing world. Moving between architecture, museums, publishing and cultural discourse, Molinari brings a deeply human perspective to the built environment — seeing design not only as something we create, but as something that shapes the way we live, connect and belong.

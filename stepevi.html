MARKA x STEPEVI

A Shared Vision. A Global Journey.

MARKA London marks the beginning of an exclusive global partnership between MARKA and STEPEVI, bringing together two worlds connected by a shared belief in creativity, design, culture and the power of ideas.

As MARKA begins its international journey, STEPEVI becomes its Exclusive Global Partner. More than a sponsor or host, STEPEVI joins MARKA as an intellectual and cultural partner, contributing its international ecosystem, perspective and creative point of view to the platform.

Through its global community of clients, designers, architects, creative collaborators and industry leaders, STEPEVI will help enrich the conversations and connections that define MARKA’s international editions.

London is a particularly meaningful starting point. It was here, in 2004, that STEPEVI began its own international journey, establishing the foundations of what has since become a global luxury design brand.

The partnership reflects the enduring commitment to design, craftsmanship, sustainability, innovation and contemporary luxury of STEPEVI; values that have shaped the brand for more than two decades.

MARKA × STEPEVI is a partnership designed to travel. Beginning in London, it will continue as MARKA expands its international journey to leading cities around the world.
